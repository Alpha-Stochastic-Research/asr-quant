## Authorship note

Software contribution and manuscript authorship are tracked separately. Inclusion in this file and in the software citation metadata recognizes contributions to the ASRQuant software release; it does not by itself assign authorship of a separate research manuscript.

## How to cite

Use `CITATION.cff` for the software release. If a specific ASRQuant paper or research note materially supports your work, cite that publication separately according to its own author list and publication metadata.
